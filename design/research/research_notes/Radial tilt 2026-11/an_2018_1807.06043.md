# An et al. 2018, surface trap with DC-tunable ion-electrode distance

**Citation.** Da An, Clemens Matthiesen, Ahmed Abdelrahman, Maya Berlin-Udi, Dylan Gorman, Sönke Möller, Erik Urban, Hartmut Häffner, "Surface trap with dc-tunable ion-electrode distance", arXiv:1807.06043v1 [quant-ph], 18 Jul 2018. Journal reference not checked [verify].

- Abstract p.1: "We investigate micromotion in the vertical direction and show cooling of both the planar and vertical motional modes into the ground state."
- Sec. II p.2, Fig. 1(c): the four-RF-electrode trap has an rf-null axis perpendicular to the surface at x = y = 0.
- Sec. II p.2: the degeneracy between the two planar modes is lifted with a DC quadrupole, giving typical |ω_x − ω_y|/ω_x ~ 0.1. "This ability ... allows us to slightly tilt the vertical mode into the trap plane" [verify wording], so the in-plane 397 nm beam projects on the near-vertical mode.
- Sec. II p.2: ion height is found by maximising fluorescence in the vertical direction, then translating the 397 nm beam until it scatters from the ion [verify].
- Sec. II p.2: secular frequencies 0.4 to 1.2 MHz radial (ω_z) and 0.6 to 2.0 MHz in-plane [verify].
