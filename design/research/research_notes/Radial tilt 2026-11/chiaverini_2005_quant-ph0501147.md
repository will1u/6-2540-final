# Chiaverini et al. 2005, surface-electrode architecture

**Citation.** J. Chiaverini, R. B. Blakestad, J. Britton, J. D. Jost, C. Langer, D. Leibfried, R. Ozeri, D. J. Wineland, "Surface-Electrode Architecture for Ion-Trap Quantum Information Processing", Quantum Information and Computation, Vol. 5, No. 6 (2005) 419-439, Rinton Press; arXiv:quant-ph/0501147 (v2). The journal line is from the PDF page header.

- Sec. 1 p.419-420: motivation for planar traps; Fig. 1 p.420 is a schematic of the five-wire layout with cooling and qubit light drawn across the electrode plane. It is not a discussion of tilted axes.
- Sec. 2 p.421 Eqs. (1)-(4): pseudopotential and the radial secular motion in a standard quadrupole. Useful for the notation of the radial modes, not for the tilt.
- Not read: Sec. 3 onward, where the five-wire geometry and its trap depth are discussed. The axis-rotation question was not checked in those pages.
