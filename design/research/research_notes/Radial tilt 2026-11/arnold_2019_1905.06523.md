# Arnold et al. 2019, branching ratios for 6P1/2 decays in 138Ba+

**Citation.** K. J. Arnold, S. R. Chanu, R. Kaewuam, T. R. Tan, L. Yeo, Zhiqiang Zhang, M. S. Safronova, M. D. Barrett, "Measurements of the branching ratios for 6P1/2 decays in 138Ba+", arXiv:1905.06523v3 (22 Oct 2019). Journal reference not on the pages read [verify].

- Abstract p.1: decay probability from 6P1/2 to 5D3/2 is p = 0.268177(37)_stat(20)_sys; the 6P1/2 lifetime τ = 7.855(10) ns [verify on the published version].
- Introduction p.1: 6P1/2 lifetime and the 493 nm (S1/2-P1/2) and 650 nm (P1/2-D3/2) transitions, Fig. 1(a).
- Sec. II p.2: weighted mean of the data p = 0.267979(21), reduced χ² = 0.80 (before the systematic term) [verify].

**Derived in a script (team calc, check before use):**
- Total excited-state rate Γ = 1/τ, so Γ/2π = 20.26 MHz from τ = 7.855 ns.
- Partial 493 nm channel (1 − p)Γ/2π = 14.83 MHz.
- Doppler limit ħΓ/2k_B for the total Γ at Γ/2π = 20.26 MHz: about 0.49 mK.

The value 15.3 MHz in the search excerpt was not reproduced from this paper's numbers and was not confirmed.
