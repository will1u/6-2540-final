**Citation.** D. J. Berkeland, J. D. Miller, J. C. Bergquist, W. M. Itano, D. J. Wineland, "Minimization of ion micromotion in a Paul trap", J. Appl. Phys. 83, 5025-5033 (1998), DOI 10.1063/1.367318 (from the title page).

- Abstract p.5025: three detection methods are described: change of the average ion position with trap potentials; sideband amplitudes of a narrow transition; Doppler-induced modulation of fluorescence on a broad transition.
