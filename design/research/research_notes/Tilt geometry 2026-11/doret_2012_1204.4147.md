## Where to read

- Design section (the RF electrode shape and the DC electrode layout): the abstract says RF electrode shapes were optimised to minimise pseudopotential perturbation. Find the figure of the electrode layout.
- The section on tuning secular frequencies and the trap-axis orientation, which the abstract says DC electrodes can adjust.
- The stray-field section and the nulling-potential section, for the cost of the compensation.

## Per-source numbers

- **Tilt angle reached:** the abstract says the DC electrodes can adjust the orientation of the trap axes; no angle given. [verify]
- **How:** 44 individually controlled DC electrodes; RF electrodes shaped by optimisation; on-chip capacitors for RF pickup. Whether the centre DC is split is not stated in the abstract.
- **Voltages, ion height, frequencies:** not stated in the abstract.
