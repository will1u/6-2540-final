## Per-source numbers (all [verify], search excerpt only)

- **Tilt angle reached:** principal radial axes rotated to 20° and 70° from the z-axis (search excerpt). [verify]
- **How:** the junction geometry itself tilts the principal axes and lifts degeneracy; no separate tilt electrode. [verify]
- **Voltages:** about ±10 V on the electrodes. [verify]
- **Mode splitting:** radial modes at 6.06 MHz split to 6.24 and 5.54 MHz (search excerpt). [verify]
- **Ion height and RF frequency:** not read.
