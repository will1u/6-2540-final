## Where to read

- Sec. "SE multipoles", "Parametrization of SE multipoles", "Field of SE multipoles", "2D multipole expansion": the multipole parametrization for strip electrodes. [verify section numbers]
- Eq. (20): the equidistant strip electrodes parametrised by offset θ₀ and width θ_w. Note it covers only symmetric arrangements. [verify]
- Eq. (23): multipole coefficient with the phase factor e^{-inθ₀}; the text says the phase describes the orientation of the multipole. This is the formal statement that rotates the axes. [verify]
- Eq. (25): quadrupole strength q against sin θ_w. [verify]
- Sec. "Depth of SE multipole traps", "Intrinsic depth" (Eq. 28; Figs. 9-10) and "Depth in the presence of static fields" (Eq. 31-32; Fig. 11): control fields and the bias on the RF electrodes. [verify]

## Per-source numbers

- **Tilt angle reached:** the paper does not treat tilted principal axes or asymmetric electrodes under control fields (per the summary). No tilt number.
- **How:** not a tilt paper; the phase in Eq. (23) is the orientation parameter.
- **Voltages:** the optimal RF-electrode bias v_c = 0.18 (Fig. 11 caption), giving a depth increase by a factor 9.8 for θ₀ = 100°, θ_w = π/2. Stability limit |v_c| ≲ 0.6 sin θ_w (no equation number). [verify]
- **Ion height and frequencies:** not given in the summary.

## Costs

- Biasing the RF electrodes with a DC offset aligns the principal axes of the control and RF quadrupoles and lets the trap depth change by a large factor (Fig. 11). This is a route to tilt-free cost, but it is a bias on the RF rails, not a tilt, and it is stated for a different geometry. [verify]
- The static-field depth result states any control field must vanish at the trap centre to avoid micromotion. [verify]
